**Figure 1. Roost links literature-derived host-factor evidence, H5N1
surveillance, and comparative genomics across 2,048 indexed
species.** (A) Three previously disconnected data streams are integrated:
1,366 papers from 22 sources are converted to structured evidence
by language-model agents (findings, typed claims, mechanisms, species links;
Methods); 44,871 H5N1 detections across 106 countries
(718 species with recorded detections) contribute
species-by-occurrence evidence; and 3,894 catalogued genome
assemblies (2,388 screened to date) contribute gene
content. Together these index 2,048 species, 4,277 host
genes (985 studied in two or more papers), and 2,951,184
gene–genome assessments. (B) One record in full:
avian ANP32A carries a 33-amino-acid insertion that mammals lack, with
evidence for a role in influenza polymerase compatibility and host range;
full-length ANP32A is detected in 1,870 of 2,337 assemblies
assessed, traced to 41 source-linked papers (594
extracted mentions; 10 carry typed studied/perturbed evidence,
7 mechanism edges). (C) The live resource — the ANP32 explorer
at https://josephguhlin.com/Roost/#/anp32 (① comparative genomics: the insert measured per species;
② mechanism) and the ANP32A record view (③ source evidence). All numbers are
generated from the same artifacts as the live site (data freeze 2026-10-29).

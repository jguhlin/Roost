**Figure 2. Synthesis over 1,366 papers
reveals both deep and fragmented host-gene evidence.**
(A) Evidence depth per host gene (3,502 study-subject genes;
registry counts; log scale; integer bins 1–19, then ≥20): 2,517
(71.9%) appear in exactly one
paper and only 985 in two or more; the most-studied genes
(IFNB1, TNF, IFNG, IL6, MX1, DDX58) each rest on 83–102 papers.
(B) The 299 reviewed mechanism edges classify into six recurrent
modules (261 edges in-module); innate sensing is the deepest
(64 papers, 102 edges).
(C) Positive control — Roost recovers the canonical ANP32–PB2 species
barrier end-to-end: avian ANP32A (33-aa insertion) binds and supports the
avian polymerase (10 reviewed evidence quotes on the
ANP32A–PB2 edge), and the mammalian-adaptation markers PB2-E627K
(179 papers), D701N (115) and
the cattle-lineage M631L (52) are each recoverable
with paper-level provenance.
(D) DDX58 (RIG-I) detection per bird order, by how the call was
made (best call per species, 431 screened H5N1-host species
across 15 orders; mammals excluded — the avian-reference
search yields fragmentary mammalian alignments). **Via annotation**
(69 species): an NCBI gene record exists under DDX58 or a
synonym (RIGI/RIG-I — NCBI records RIG-I as RIGI in many taxa, which
exact-symbol matching missed); the annotation's judgement is used as-is.
**BLAST, annotation misses it** (119): full-length search hit
in a species whose NCBI annotation exists but does not include RIG-I.
**BLAST, no annotation** (170): full-length hit in a species
with no NCBI gene records — putative. Plus fragmentary hits and species
where the gene was searched for and not found. Galliformes: 16 of
19 species not found (15 high-confidence absences; sole
detection: the basally divergent
Alectura lathami), versus
≤17% not-found in every other order
(Anseriformes 2 of 99;
5 orders 0). In the literature,
7 of the 64 sensing-module papers report
species differences, including chicken RIG-I (DDX58)/RNF135 loss
(BMC Genomics (2024)) and the K172-deficient duck RIG-I
(Avian Immunology (2022)); chickens retain the compensatory sensor
MDA5 (IFIH1). The pattern is visible only when literature and genomes are
read together. All numbers are generated from the same artifacts as the
live site (data freeze 2026-10-29); annotation states from the alias-aware
pipeline (gene_config aliases + NCBI taxon re-check).
